Unofficial Verbier

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unofficial Verbier handles information when you use the Unofficial Verbier mobile application. Unofficial Verbier is an independent guide and is not an official application of Verbier Tourism or the resort.

Information we collect

If you create an account, the app may collect your email address, an optional display name, an internal user identifier, saved places, and an optional Skiline handle. If you choose to enable mountain-warning notifications, a push-notification token may also be associated with your account.

How we use information

We use this information to provide authentication and account functionality, synchronize saved places and preferences, maintain app security, provide account support, and deliver mountain-warning notifications when you have opted in.

Location

The app may request foreground location permission when you choose to use the location control on the mountain map. Your location is used transiently on your device to centre the map. Unofficial Verbier does not store or receive your precise or coarse location coordinates. Map providers may receive the requested map area and ordinary network information necessary to return map content.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you opt in to mountain warnings, the app may process a push token through Expo and the relevant device push-notification service so notifications can be delivered. You can change notification permissions through your device settings.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate app functionality. These include Supabase for backend, authentication and account data, Expo for application and notification services, and the applicable device push service. These providers process information as necessary to provide their services. We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

Analytics and advertising

The current app does not use a dedicated advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising.

Data security

Data transmitted between the app and its backend services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and account deletion

Account-related information is retained as needed to provide your account and app functionality. You can request deletion from within the app through Profile → Delete account. Account deletion removes the associated authentication user, profile, saved places, optional Skiline handle, warning device registration and linked notification deliveries.

You can also access the account deletion information page.

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account where account functionality is not required, decline location permission, decline notifications, or revoke permissions later through your device settings.

Teenage users and children

Unofficial Verbier is a general-audience destination and mountain guide intended for users aged 13 and over, including teenagers and adults. The app is not primarily designed for children. Core destination information can be used without creating an account.

Account creation is optional. If a teenage user creates an account, the app may process the same limited account information described in this policy, including an email address, optional display name, internal user identifier, saved places, optional Skiline handle, and, if enabled, a push-notification token. This information is used only for the purposes described in this policy and is not used for third-party advertising.

Where applicable law requires consent from a parent or legal guardian before a minor can provide personal information or use account features, that consent should be obtained before creating an account. If a parent or guardian believes that a minor has provided personal information without required consent, they may contact us through the developer contact information on the app store listing or use the account deletion options described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Unofficial Verbier, please contact the app operator through the developer contact information provided on the app's Google Play or Apple App Store listing.
