Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Unofficial Verbier is an independent, unofficial destination guide for Verbier, Switzerland. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Verbier Tourisme, Téléverbier or 4 Vallées. The data controller is the Unofficial Verbier publisher identified on the App Store and Google Play listing.

Information we process

Account data includes your email address, authentication records, display name, optional Skiline handle and saved places. If you enable mountain-warning notifications, we process an Expo push token, language, update time and delivery status. The app also keeps preferences and short-lived caches on your device.

To understand and improve use of the service, we process a randomly generated installation identifier, session identifiers, app version, platform, feature views and interactions with business listings. Business QR links record the campaign visited and can be associated with an app open. If you submit an issue report, we receive its message, your optional reply email, app version, platform and operating-system version. A random reporting identifier is used to limit spam and removed after 24 hours. We do not store precise location, advertising identifiers or search text as analytics data, and we do not sell personal information.

Launch offer and subscriptions

When you request or recover the winter launch offer, your email address is processed to create a keyed lookup value. The pass database retains that lookup rather than your readable email, together with the pass dates and status, a device public key and fingerprint, and an account identifier when available. This supports access, recovery and prevention of duplicate claims; it is not a mailing-list registration. The private signing key stays on the device.

Paid subscriptions use RevenueCat and the relevant app store to process purchase and entitlement information associated with an app user identifier. We use this information to provide, restore and verify paid access. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Store transaction records are subject to the store and service providers’ retention rules.

The configured winter-offer retention schedule removes pass, email-lookup and associated device-binding records 180 days after expiry, and campaign audit records after 365 days. Temporary challenge and abuse-prevention records have shorter lifetimes. You can request access or deletion through the developer support contact; a hashed lookup is still treated as personal data. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove a device credential retained by the operating system.

Purposes and legal bases

We use account information to provide the service you request; consent for optional notifications; legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention, quota protection, reliability and aggregate service and business-performance analytics; and legal obligations where applicable.

Processors and external services

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage and server functions. Expo and Apple Push Notification Service or Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver opted-in notifications. Google Maps Platform handles map and place requests. Weather, transport, mountain and webcam providers may receive ordinary network information when requests reach them. Providers may process information outside Switzerland or your country under their contractual safeguards and applicable transfer mechanisms.

Permissions and location

Notifications are optional. Location is optional and requested only when you press the locate button on the mountain map. Your position is used transiently on your device to centre the map and is not stored by Unofficial Verbier. Map providers receive the requested map area and ordinary network information needed to return map tiles. Opening directions may send a place or route to the map service you choose.

Retention

Account, profile, Skiline and saved-place records remain until account deletion. Push-device records are removed when withdrawn, on account deletion, or after 90 days without an update. Notification delivery records are removed after 30 days. Completed or rejected web deletion requests are removed after 30 days. Issue reports are deleted after 12 months. Raw pseudonymous analytics and QR visit records are retained for up to 25 months; aggregate reporting records may be retained longer for season-to-season comparison. Local caches expire according to their source licence or when the app is removed.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, unlink Skiline, remove saved places, disable notifications and delete your account. Depending on applicable law, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to processing or complain to a data-protection authority.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can choose Delete account in Profile for immediate deletion. If you cannot access the app, use the public deletion-request page. Web requests are verified and normally completed within 30 days.

Security and contact

Private credentials stay server-side and account-owned rows use database row-level security. For privacy questions, use our support form.

Map data and licences

Mountain-map data is derived from OpenSkiData / OpenSkiMap, including OpenStreetMap contributors, Skimap.org and Who’s On First. The database is adapted for the Verbier area. OpenStreetMap-derived database material is subject to the Open Database License (ODbL). See OpenSkiMap and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Source, basemap and terrain credits remain available at the bottom of the map.

The map-data licence applies to the relevant geographic database, not to your personal information or the app’s source code.
