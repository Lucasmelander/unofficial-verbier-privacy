Delete Your Account

Account and data deletion information

Users of the Unofficial Verbier mobile application can permanently delete their account and associated account data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Unofficial Verbier.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account.

This is the primary way to request deletion of your Unofficial Verbier account and associated account data.

Data deleted with your account

Account deletion removes the associated authentication user and account data, including your profile, saved places, optional Skiline handle, mountain-warning device registration and linked notification deliveries.

Data retention

Unofficial Verbier does not intentionally retain the account data listed above after the account-deletion process has been completed, except where retention is required by applicable law, security obligations, or necessary short-term technical backup processes. Any such retained information is kept only for the period required for that purpose and is not used to continue providing the deleted account.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access your account or the in-app deletion option, contact the app operator using the developer contact information shown on the Unofficial Verbier Google Play or Apple App Store listing and request deletion of your Unofficial Verbier account and associated data.

Privacy

For more information about how Unofficial Verbier handles personal information, see the Unofficial Verbier Privacy Policy.
