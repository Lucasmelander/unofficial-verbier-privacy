Delete your Unofficial Verbier account

Last updated 1 October 2026

If you can sign in, open Profile → Delete account for immediate deletion of your account and associated Unofficial Verbier data.

Request deletion without the app

Enter the email address used for your Unofficial Verbier account. We will verify the request before deletion and normally complete it within 30 days.

Deletion removes the Auth account, profile, Skiline handle, saved places, warning push token and linked delivery records. Completed request records are removed after 30 days. Information is retained longer only when a documented legal obligation requires it.
